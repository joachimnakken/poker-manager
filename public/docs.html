01

What it is

Everything a home game needs a tournament director for, on one screen. Put a laptop or tablet at the end of the table, start the clock, and tap KO as players bust.

A tournament moves through five states

setup → running ⇆ paused ⇆ break → finished

The state drives what you can do. Players can only be removed in setup. Knockouts and rebuys only work once play has begun. Finished swaps the whole view for the results screen.

02

Starting a tournament

  1. On the home page, give the tournament a name. Leave it blank and it takes today's date — "Aug 17 Poker Night".
  2. Pick a date. Defaults to today.
  3. Hit Create Tournament and you land straight on the tournament screen.

Come back to the home page later and every tournament you've run is listed newest-first, with its player count and a status badge. Delete removes one for good.

Got the name wrong? Click the pencil next to the title on the tournament page and type over it. Enter saves, Esc cancels.

03

Adding players

+ Add Player opens a box that wants one thing: a name. Add everyone, then start.

  • Players can only be removed while the tournament is still in setup. Once the clock starts, the way out is a knockout.
  • The Start Tournament button stays disabled until at least two players are in.
  • You can keep adding players after the tournament has started — useful for latecomers.
04

The clock

The big panel at the top. It shows the level you're on, the blinds, the countdown, and what's coming next.

Reading it

Above the countdown: the current level number and blinds — or just BREAK if you're on one. Below it, a Next: line previews the level after this one so you can warn the table.

Controls

Prev · Play/Pause · Next
The three round buttons under the clock. Prev and Next jump a level and reset that level's clock to its full duration.
Space
Pauses and resumes without reaching for the mouse. Ignored while you're typing in a field.
Reset round
The small circular arrow beside the countdown. It only appears while paused, and puts the current level's full time back on the clock.
Restart tournament
The circular arrow in the top-right corner of the panel. Wipes the timer, knockouts, rebuys and addons and drops back to setup. It asks for confirmation first.

It runs itself

  • At zero the clock advances to the next level on its own and keeps running — including into and out of breaks. It only stops at the end of the structure.
  • One beep at 60 seconds left, and a rising three-tone chime on every level change.
  • The browser tab title mirrors the clock — 200/400 12:34 - Poker — so a tab in the background still tells you where you are.
  • The screen stays awake while the timer runs, and re-arms if you switch away and come back. No more tapping a sleeping tablet mid-hand.
05

Knocking players out

KO next to a player opens a short list of everyone still in — pick whoever took their chips. Two taps, and the bookkeeping is done.

  • The eliminated player drops down the list, greyed out and tagged OUT, with their finishing position and "by <name>" underneath.
  • Finish position counts down. Bust the 7th of 7 players and they get #7; the next one out gets #6.
  • Undo Last Knockout reverses the most recent one if you tapped the wrong name.
  • When one player is left the tournament finishes by itself and that player takes 1st. You never have to declare a winner.

Recording who did the knocking isn't just bookkeeping — knockout counts show up on the podium at the end, which settles the bounty argument before it starts.

06

Rebuys and addons

Rebuy

  • Available only up to the last rebuy level — level 4 out of the box. After that the button disappears for everyone.
  • Breaks don't count toward the cutoff. The break sitting after level 4 still shows the button; it goes away when play resumes at level 6.
  • Rebuying a player who is already out brings them back into the tournament and renumbers everyone else's finishing position. It's the only action that can un-eliminate someone.

Addon

  • Once per player, and the button vanishes after they've taken it.
  • No level window — it's offered any time before the tournament finishes, including during setup.
  • Only offered to players who are still in.

Both feed the prize pool the moment you tap them. The stats bar and the Payouts tab recalculate immediately, so the pot on screen is always the pot on the table.

Players carry small badges in the list: IN or OUT, R:2 for two rebuys, A for an addon.

07

The four tabs

Beside the player list, four tabs hold everything else worth looking at.

TabWhat it shows
Blinds The full structure top to bottom. The current level is highlighted and levels already played are dimmed, so a glance tells you how far in you are.
Payouts Buy-ins, rebuys and addons itemised, the total pot, then each paid position with its percentage. Once a position is decided the winner's name appears beside it.
Knockouts Every elimination, newest first, with finishing position, the level it happened on, and who did it.
Seating Draw Seats assigns everyone a random seat number. Redraw reshuffles, Clear removes them.
08

The stats bar

Four numbers sitting between the clock and the tables, updating as you go.

Players
Still in, out of the total who bought in — 5/9.
Avg Stack
Every chip in play divided by the players left, so it climbs as people bust. Buy-ins, rebuys and addons all count.
Prize Pool
The live total, in your chosen currency.
Rebuys / Addons
How many of each have been taken across the table.
09

Settings

Settings in the header, available any time before the tournament finishes. Changes apply immediately — there's no save button.

General

Tournament name, and the currency label used everywhere money is shown (kr by default).

Buy-in & chips

Buy-in, starting chips, last rebuy level, rebuy amount and chips, addon amount and chips. The money fields drive the prize pool; the chip fields drive the average stack.

Blind structure

  • Edit small blind, big blind, ante and minutes on any row, in place.
  • + Level appends a level with the last one's blinds doubled.
  • + Break appends a 10-minute break.
  • X deletes a row.
  • Reset to Defaults restores the shipped 18-level structure.

Structure edits apply to this tournament only. Build one you like, then use Play Again at the end to carry it into the next game.

10

When it's over

The moment the last knockout lands, the tournament view is replaced by the results screen.

  • Prize pool summary — the total, with a line counting the players, rebuys and addons that built it.
  • Podium — 2nd, 1st and 3rd on stepped blocks, each with their payout and how many knockouts they took.
  • Final standings — everyone in order. Paid places show their money; everyone else shows the level they went out on and who did it.

Play Again clones the tournament: same settings, same blind structure, same player names, today's date, all stats cleared. You land straight in the new one, ready to deal.

12

Good to know

Three things worth knowing before the first game

It lives in this browser
Tournaments are saved locally, so they survive a refresh, a closed lid or a crash. But they don't sync anywhere — another device won't see them, and clearing site data deletes them. Run the game from one machine.
Rebuying a bust player renumbers everyone
It's the correct behaviour, but it's startling if you meant to hit Addon. The finishing order of everyone already out shifts to make room for the player coming back in.
Sound needs one click first
Browsers block audio until you've interacted with the page. Starting the tournament counts, so in practice the beeps just work — but if you loaded the page and never clicked, the first one is silent.
